# Anh Nguyen Ba Hoang

PhD student in Data Science at **Rowan University**, advised by Prof. Dat Ba Tran.

I work on **bilevel optimization and machine learning**, particularly learning with noisy labels and imbalanced data. I am also interested in applications of optimization and statistical learning to quantitative research.

## Selected research

- **BiKD — Bilevel Knowledge Distillation**  
  First-author paper at **PAKDD 2026**. Adaptive sample-wise weighting of hard and soft distillation losses for long-tailed classification.  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17839) · [Published version](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-92-1926-1_30) · [Official code](https://github.com/phan-tho/Bilevel-balancing-kd)

- **EBOMLC — Efficient Bilevel Optimization for Meta Label Correction**  
  First-author work on efficient learning with noisy labels. Preprint under minor revision at *Machine Learning*.  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17833) · [Code](https://github.com/hoanganhknk/EBOMLC)

Across these projects, my contributions span problem formulation, algorithm design, theoretical analysis, implementation, and experiments.
